Common Questions

Straight answers to what small business owners ask us most.

What does it cost?

We don't do enterprise pricing. Every business is different, so we quote each job after a quick, free intro call once we understand your size and setup. There's no charge to talk and no obligation — most small businesses are surprised how affordable practical security can be.

Do you need access to my passwords or systems?

Only what's needed for the work you've approved, and always with your permission. We follow least-privilege practices, document what we access, and never ask for more than the job requires. You stay in control the whole time.

Is my business data kept confidential?

Yes. Anything we see during an assessment stays private. We're happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before we start, and we don't store sensitive information about your business beyond what's needed to deliver your report.

How long does an assessment take?

For most small businesses, a security checkup takes a few days from kickoff to a finished report. Larger or more complex setups take a little longer. We'll give you a clear timeline before any work begins.

Do you just hand me a report, or do you fix things?

Your choice. Every assessment ends with a prioritized, plain-language action plan. We can fix the issues for you, walk your team through the fixes, or hand the report to your existing IT person — whatever works best for you.

Do I need to be a tech expert to work with you?

Not at all. We built HiohSec for businesses that don't have an IT department. We keep things simple and walk you through every step in plain language. We also work with clients in Spanish, so let us know what you prefer. Tambien atendemos en espanol.
